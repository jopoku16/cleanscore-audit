# CleanScore deviation log

This log lists, in date order, every point where what was done differed from, added to, or went wrong against a registration for the CleanScore audit and its positive-control studies. It replaces the private working log, which cannot be published. All dates are 2026. Each row gives what happened and its effect on the reported results. A language model drafted the fresh forms and control banks.

| Date | What happened | Effect on the reported results |
|---|---|---|
| 21 Aug | Banks built as registered. 12 ARC rewrites failed the dissimilarity check (token Jaccard 0.80 or higher) or marginally exceeded the length band; all were rewritten before any model was queried. | None; no model had been queried. |
| 21 Aug | OLMo-2-7B ran out of GPU memory at generation batch 4; rerun at batch 2. Amendment 1, declared before the Qwen reruns started: both Qwen models hit the 320-token cap on 40.7% and 29.8% of GSM8K forms, so they were rerun at 768 tokens with a parser that also accepts a final `\boxed{}`. Saved continuations were clipped to 600 characters, so offline re-parsing was impossible; runtime scores used full text and are valid. | OLMo-2-7B: no scoring change. Registered result stays primary; Amendment 1 reported beside it: truncation fell to zero, GSM8K gaps -0.040 and -0.008, neither excluding zero. ARC results identical form by form (determinism check). |
